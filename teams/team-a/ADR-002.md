# ADR-002 — AskIT v1 retrieval settings

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Status | Proposed |
| Writer | Team A |
| Presenter | Team A |
| Date | 2026-10-05 |

## Context
AskIT is a support assistant for employees asking operational questions about VPN, MFA, lost devices, account lockouts, and other workplace IT issues. Our v1 retrieval pipeline needs to be fast enough for live chat while still returning the right KB article and a trustworthy answer.

## Decision
We choose a chunk size of 300 words, overlap of 100 words, and Top-K of 4 for AskIT v1. This gave the strongest overall balance in our mini-eval: 9/10 document hits and 7/10 answer hits, which was better than the 800/150/4 setting (8/10 doc, 6/10 answer) and the 150/50/4 setting (8/10 doc, 6/10 answer).

### What we give up
We accept a small accuracy tradeoff to keep latency and cost in check. This setup is more expensive and slower than a very small index, but it is still fast enough for a human support workflow and more accurate than a coarse 800/150/2 configuration (7/10 doc hit, 5/10 answer hit). In short: we are optimizing for accuracy first, with cost and speed kept reasonable rather than minimal.

## Why this choice
- Smaller chunks keep each retrieval unit focused on a single topic, which helps with lockout, VPN, and MFA questions.
- A moderate overlap protects context near chunk boundaries without creating too much repeated text.
- Top-K of 4 gives enough candidate passages to cover the likely answer while staying lean enough to keep response time down.

## Risks
- Risk 1: A very specific question may still miss the exact KB passage even with 300/100/4, especially when wording differs from the source article.
- Risk 2: More chunks increase index size and embedding cost compared with a larger chunk strategy.
- Risk 3: A retrieval hit can still be semantically nearby but not legally or operationally precise enough for a support answer.

## Question the app still gets wrong
A question like: "Can a contractor use the VPN without a full employee account?" is still a weak spot for this v1 setup. The app may retrieve an article about VPN access but miss the nuance about contractor eligibility or required approval.

## What we would try next (Day 4)
We would add an agentic critic/rewrite loop: first retrieve candidate chunks, then ask a second model to decide whether the answer is complete and, if not, rewrite the search query and re-run retrieval. This matches the next lab’s direction and should improve multi-part or ambiguous questions without blowing up the total cost.

## Experiment evidence
| chunk size | overlap | top-K | doc hit | answer hit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 800 | 150 | 4 | 8/10 | 6/10 |
| 300 | 100 | 4 | 9/10 | 7/10 |
| 150 | 50 | 4 | 8/10 | 6/10 |
| 800 | 150 | 2 | 7/10 | 5/10 |

## Final recommendation
For AskIT v1, keep 300/100/4 and measure the live app against a few real user questions. If the support team reports many “almost right but missing the nuance” cases, we should move to the Day 4 agentic loop rather than increasing Top-K alone.
